# Project brief

## Purpose and audience

A professional portfolio for Aagam Shah, aimed at employers, clients, and collaborators seeking an IT Support Engineer or Web Developer in Mumbai. It presents a combined practice spanning hardware, systems, networks, support, hosting, and websites.

## Goals

- Make the breadth of technical work clear within the first screen.
- Show real experience and selected work with links to live websites.
- Let a visitor download the resume or prepare an email enquiry.
- Keep future content additions and visual changes simple.

## Design philosophy

Dark, precise, editorial and technical. The low-poly workstation symbolizes support and web infrastructure. Motion provides hierarchy; text and actions remain available before WebGL loads.

## Constraints

Use only facts from `Aagam_Shah_Resume_2026.pdf`. Do not fabricate outcomes, metrics, technologies per individual company website, credential links, or social accounts. Keep mobile, keyboard use, reduced motion, SEO, and performance in scope. The contact form uses the visitor's email app until a backend is configured.
